## Your idea
My product helps a developer get their pull request reviewed faster. When I open a PR, it reads my changes and gives me a short, clear summary, plus a list of the risky parts that the reviewer should check first. So the reviewer doesn’t have to guess where the problems might be, and I don’t wait days for feedback.
